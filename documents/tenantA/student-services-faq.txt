OFFICES AND HOURS
==================================================================

Q1: What time does the Registrar's Office open?
A: The Office of the University Registrar (Agila Hall Room 101) is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and stays open through lunch. During enrollment periods it is also open on Saturdays, 8:00 AM to 12:00 NN.

Q2: What time does the Cashier close?
A: The Cashier (Agila Hall Room 103) is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. It closes one hour earlier than the Registrar. During enrollment periods it is also open on Saturdays, 8:00 AM to 12:00 NN.

Q3: Where is the Office of Student Affairs?
A: Kalapati Hall Room 201. It is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Email osa@bagwisu.example or call local 201.

Q4: Where is the scholarship office?
A: The Office of Scholarships and Financial Aid (OSFA) is at Kalapati Hall Room 204, open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Email scholarships@bagwisu.example.

Q5: Is any office open on Sundays?
A: Only Campus Security at Gate 1, which is open 24 hours. All other offices and the Banog Library are closed on Sundays and holidays.

Q6: What is the university's phone number?
A: The trunk line is (02) 8555-0100. Add the local number of the office you need, for example local 101 for the Registrar.

==================================================================
STUDENT ID
==================================================================

Q7: I forgot my ID. Can I still enter the campus?
A: Yes. Ask Campus Security at Gate 1 for a temporary gate pass. You can do this only 3 times per semester.

Q8: How do I replace a lost ID?
A: Go to the Office of Student Affairs with an affidavit of loss and pay ₱300 at the Cashier. Your new ID will be released within five working days.

Q9: What is the ID validation sticker?
A: A sticker placed on your ID every semester to show you are enrolled. Get it at the OSA by showing your Certificate of Registration (COR). You need it to enter the campus after the second week of classes.

Q10: How is my student number formatted?
A: YYYY-NNNNN: the year you entered Bagwis, then five digits.

==================================================================
CLINIC AND HEALTH
==================================================================

Q11: What are the clinic hours?
A: The University Clinic (Maya Hall ground floor) is open Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Q12: Do I have to pay for a check-up at the clinic?
A: No. Consultations, first aid and basic medicines are free for enrolled students.

Q13: Does the clinic have a dentist?
A: Yes. Dental services are available on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 8:00 AM to 12:00 NN, by appointment. Email clinic@bagwisu.example to book.

Q14: How do I get a medical certificate for my absence?
A: The clinic issues one if you were seen at the clinic, or if you bring a certificate from an outside doctor within 3 school days after you return to class.

Q15: Does an excused absence still count as an absence?
A: Yes. Excused absences still count toward the 20% absence limit, but your instructor must let you make up missed quizzes and exams.

==================================================================
GUIDANCE AND COUNSELING
==================================================================

Q16: How do I talk to a counselor?
A: Walk in at the Guidance and Counseling Center, Kalapati Hall Room 210, or book by emailing guidance@bagwisu.example. It is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday, 8:00 AM to 12:00 NN.

Q17: Is counseling confidential?
A: Yes. What you share with a counselor is kept confidential.

Q18: Who do I call if I need urgent help at night?
A: Go to the University Clinic if it is open, or call Campus Security at local 199. Security is open 24 hours.

Q19: Do graduating students need to visit Guidance?
A: Yes. The exit interview at the Guidance and Counseling Center is a graduation requirement.

==================================================================
LIBRARY
==================================================================

Q20: What are the library hours?
A: Banog Library is open Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM to 8:00 PM, and Saturday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. It is closed on Sundays and holidays. During final exam weeks it stays open until 10:00 PM on weekdays.

Q21: How many books can I borrow?
A: Undergraduates can borrow 3 books for 7 days. Graduate students can borrow 5 books for 14 days. You can renew a book once if nobody has reserved it.

Q22: How much is the library fine?
A: ₱10 per day for each overdue book. Overdue reserve books are fined ₱20 per hour.

Q23: What if I lose a library book?
A: You pay the replacement cost of the book plus a ₱200 processing fee.

Q24: Can I eat in the library?
A: No food is allowed. Drinks are allowed only in covered bottles.

Q25: How much does printing cost?
A: Printing at the library costs ₱3 per page for black and white and ₱10 per page for color.

DOCUMENTS
==================================================================

Q31: How do I request my transcript of records?
A: File a request at the Registrar (Agila Hall Room 101) or through "Document Request" on BagwisNet. It costs ₱150 per page and takes 5 to 7 working days. You must have no unpaid balance.

Q32: How fast can I get a Certificate of Enrollment?
A: In 1 working day. It costs ₱100.

Q33: Where do I get a Certificate of Good Moral Character?
A: From the Office of Student Affairs, not the Registrar. It costs ₱150 and takes 3 working days.

Q34: Can someone else claim my documents for me?
A: Yes, with an authorization letter and the IDs of both you and your representative.

==================================================================
CAMPUS LIFE AND RULES
==================================================================

Q35: When do I have to wear the uniform?
A: Monday to Thursday. Friday is "wash day", when you may wear decent casual clothes. Slippers, shorts, sleeveless tops and ripped jeans are not allowed on any day.

Q36: How do we register a new student organization?
A: Apply for accreditation at the OSA. You need at least 15 members, a faculty adviser, a constitution and by-laws, a list of officers and a plan of activities. The deadlines are July 31, 2026 for the first semester and December 18, 2026 for the second semester.

Q37: How early must we ask approval for an event?
A: At least 10 working days before the event, through the OSA.

Q38: Are classes suspended when there is a typhoon?
A: Classes are automatically suspended when Tropical Cyclone Wind Signal No. 2 or higher is raised over Lungsod ng Sampaguita, or when there is an orange or red rainfall warning. Announcements come out on BagwisNet and the official Bagwis University page by 5:00 AM for morning classes and 11:00 AM for afternoon classes.

Q39: Where do I look for something I lost?
A: At the Lost and Found in the Security Office at Gate 1. Items are kept for 30 days, then donated.

Q40: What time do the campus gates open and close?
A: 5:30 AM to 10:00 PM every day.

Q41: Where can I eat on campus?
A: At the Kapitbahay Food Court in Maya Hall, open Monday to Saturday, 6:30 AM to 7:00 PM.

Q42: How much is a parking sticker?
A: ₱500 per semester for a car and ₱250 per semester for a motorcycle. Apply at the Campus Security Office.

CAREERS AND OTHERS

Q43: Does the university help with internships?

Q44: When is the career fair?
A: The Lipad Career Fair is on February 19, 2027.

Q45: Is there a holiday on Foundation Day?
A: Yes. There are no classes on Foundation Day, September 18, 2026.

Q46: Who do I contact about my personal data?
A: The Data Protection Officer, Atty. Noel B. Garcia, at dpo@bagwisu.example or local 115.

Q47: I was affected by a typhoon and can't pay my fees. Is there help?
A: Yes. You can apply at the OSFA for Emergency Financial Assistance, a one-time grant of up to ₱10,000, within 30 calendar days after the event. Bring a barangay certification. You may also ask about the interest-free Emergency Student Loan of up to ₱15,000.

Q48: Do I need my parents' permission for an off-campus activity?
A: Yes. Every participant needs a signed parent's consent form, and a faculty adviser must go with the group.
