## 4. Enrollment rules (Enrollment and Registration Policy v2.0)

- Document: **Enrollment and Registration Policy, Version 2.0**, code BU-OUR-POL-001, effective **April 15, 2026**, supersedes Version 1.0 (issued February 2, 2026).
- Officially enrolled = **Certificate of Registration (COR)** issued after payment and Registrar validation.
- Enrollment steps for continuing students (8 steps): (1) clear balances / get clearance, (2) check grades & curriculum evaluation on BagwisNet, (3) academic advising → advising slip, (4) online course selection on BagwisNet, (5) assessment of fees (Accounting), (6) payment (Cashier or online), (7) Registrar validation → COR, (8) ID validation sticker at OSA.
- Freshmen requirements: Notice of Admission (admission slip) from Admissions, SHS report card (Form 138), PSA birth certificate (photocopy + original for checking), Certificate of Good Moral Character, two 2x2 ID photos, medical clearance from University Clinic. Entrance test: Bagwis University College Admission Test (BUCAT), fee ₱500.
- Transferees: Honorable Dismissal (transfer credentials), copy of TOR for evaluation, Good Moral, PSA birth certificate, two 2x2 photos, medical clearance. Credit evaluation by dean; max **50%** of program units may be credited; only grades equivalent to **2.0 or better** (Bagwis scale) are credited.
- Returning students (absent 1+ semester without LOA): readmission form at Registrar + clearance.
- Unit load: regular max **24 units**; overload up to **27 units** only for graduating students with dean approval and previous semester GWA ≥ **3.00**; Academic Warning max **18 units**; Academic Probation max **15 units**; midyear max **9 units**.
- Adding/dropping: through change-of-course form, ₱100 per transaction, until add/drop deadline.
- Shifting programs: file at Registrar during enrollment; needs cumulative GWA ≥ **2.50**, approval of both deans; ₱200 fee; max **two** shifts during a student's stay.
- Cross-enrollment: max **6 units** per semester, Registrar permit required; not allowed while on probation.
- Leave of Absence (LOA): max **2 consecutive semesters**; filed by the W deadline; a student who exceeds it must apply for readmission.
- Official withdrawal from a course (grade "W"): by the W deadline (Aug 28, 2026 / Feb 12, 2027); after that, no W.

## 6. Grading and academic standing (Grading and Academic Policy)

Document: **Grading and Academic Policy**, code BU-OVPAA-POL-002, effective June 29, 2026 (AY 2026–2027), approved March 2, 2026.

### Grade scale (higher number = better)
| Grade | Percentage | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 4.0 | 96–100 | Excellent (highest) |
| 3.5 | 91–95 | Superior |
| 3.0 | 86–90 | Very Good |
| 2.5 | 81–85 | Good |
| 2.0 | 76–80 | Satisfactory |
| 1.5 | 71–75 | Fair |
| 1.0 | 65–70 | Passed (lowest passing) |
| 0.0 | below 65 | Failed |
| INC | — | Incomplete |
| W | — | Officially withdrawn (no grade value, not in GWA) |
| FA | — | Failure due to absences (= 0.0 in GWA) |

- Passing percentage: **65**.
- Term grade = 60% class standing + 40% term exam. **Final grade = 40% midterm grade + 60% final term grade.**
- Lab courses: lecture 60% + laboratory 40%.
- Final percentage rounded to nearest whole number before conversion (85.5 → 86 → 3.0).
- **GWA** = Σ(grade × units) / Σ(units). **NSTP excluded**, PE included; W and unresolved INC excluded; 0.0 and FA included. GWA is **truncated** to 2 decimals, never rounded up (3.4983 → 3.49, which does NOT reach 3.50).
- Repeated courses: both grades stay on TOR and both count in cumulative GWA.
- Grade appeal: within **10 working days** after grades are posted: instructor → department chair → dean (dean's decision final).

### INC
- Given when class standing is passing but a final exam or requirement is missing for a valid reason.
- Must be completed within **one semester**: by the **last day of final exams of the next regular semester** (midyear term does not count). Deadlines: INCs from 2nd sem 2025–26 → Oct 24, 2026; INCs from 1st sem 2026–27 → March 20, 2027.
- Not completed → **automatically becomes 0.0**.
- Process: completion form from Registrar, pay ₱150 per course at Cashier, instructor submits grade.
- Any INC in a semester → not eligible for Dean's List that semester (even if completed later).
- Graduating students must have no INC by March 20, 2027.

### Attendance
- Absences above **20%** of total class hours (e.g., more than 10.8 of 54 hours for a 3-unit course, i.e., 11 hours) → instructor may give **FA** (= 0.0).
- Late = arriving more than 15 minutes after start; after 30 minutes = absent. **3 lates = 1 absence.**
- Excused absences (clinic/OSA slip) still count toward the 20% but allow make-up work.

### Academic standing (checked after each regular semester; midyear term does not change standing but counts in cumulative GWA)
| Standing | Trigger | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| Good standing | none of the below | normal load |
| **Academic Warning** | failed units are 25% to less than 50% of enrolled units, OR semester GWA below 2.00 | max 18 units; must meet adviser |
| **Academic Probation** | failed units 50% to less than 75%, OR a second consecutive semester on Warning | max 15 units; signs probation contract; meets adviser monthly; cannot hold org office or run for BSSC; no overload; no cross-enrollment; **all university scholarships and grants suspended** (BAES, Tulong Grant, SAP, Agila Varsity, Performing Arts) |
| Lifting probation | next regular semester GWA ≥ 2.00 and no 0.0/FA | back to good standing |
| **Dismissal from program** | failed units 75% or more; OR probation not lifted; OR failing the same course 3 times | may apply once to shift to another program |
| **Debarment** (from university) | dismissed from a program a second time | cannot re-enroll |
- Ever being on Academic Probation → **not eligible for Latin honors**.

### Honors
- **Dean's List** (per semester): GWA ≥ **3.50**, at least **15 units** (excluding NSTP), no grade below **2.5**, no INC/W/0.0/FA, no disciplinary sanction that semester.
- **President's List**: GWA ≥ **3.75** + same conditions.
- **Latin honors** (cumulative GWA, excluding NSTP): Summa cum laude ≥ **3.80**; Magna cum laude **3.60–3.79**; Cum laude **3.40–3.59**. Also: no final grade below **2.0** in any course (incl. INC that became 0.0), never on probation, at least **75%** of units taken at Bagwis, finished within the regular program length plus at most 1 extra semester, no major offense.

## 7. Scholarships and financial aid (Scholarships and Financial Aid Policy)

Document: **Scholarships and Financial Aid Policy**, code BU-OSFA-POL-003, effective April 1, 2026.

| Program | Benefit | Key requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **Bagwis Academic Excellence Scholarship (BAES) – Full** | 100% tuition + ₱2,000 book allowance per semester | continuing: semester GWA ≥ **3.75**; freshmen: SHS general average ≥ **95** and passed BUCAT |
| **BAES – Partial** | 50% tuition | continuing: semester GWA **3.50–3.74**; freshmen: SHS general average **92–94** |

| **Student Assistantship Program (SAP)** | 100% tuition up to 21 units + ₱2,000 monthly allowance | GWA ≥ **2.00**; no 0.0; max load **21 units**; work **15 hours/week** in an assigned office |

| **Emergency Student Loan** | up to **₱15,000**, interest-free | pay back before final exams of same semester; co-signed by parent/guardian; no outstanding loan |

Stacking rules:
- Only **one** university scholarship or grant per semester; if qualified for two, the student keeps the **higher** benefit.
- The **sibling discount** can be combined **only with the Bagwis Tulong Grant**.
- External scholarships may be combined with a university scholarship as long as the total does not exceed **100% of tuition**.
- The 5% full-payment discount is computed on the tuition left after scholarships/grants.

Loss / suspension / renewal:
- Renewal is evaluated each semester; renewal documents due **two weeks after grades are posted** (Nov 13, 2026; April 9, 2027).
- BAES GWA falls below 3.50 or any grade below 2.5 → BAES lost for next semester; may reapply after one qualifying semester.
- BAES GWA 3.50–3.74 → full scholar becomes partial (downgrade) for next semester; GWA ≥ 3.75 → partial scholar is upgraded to full.
- INC on record → renewal **on hold**; if completed by the renewal deadline and grades qualify → kept; if not completed by renewal deadline → lost for next semester; INC turned 0.0 → lost.
- Academic Probation → **all** scholarships/grants suspended for that semester (see grading policy).
- Major disciplinary offense → scholarship revoked.
- Official withdrawal from the university → scholarship cancelled; scholarship-covered amounts are not refunded to the student.
- Appeal of loss: within **5 working days** of the notice, to the Scholarship Committee; grounds: documented medical or family emergency; only **one** granted appeal per student per scholarship.
- Applications: BagwisNet "Scholarships" tab + documents to OSFA (Kalapati Hall Rm 204); results on BagwisNet and OSFA bulletin board.
