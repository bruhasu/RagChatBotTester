# 1. About This Warranty

## 1.1 What the Warranty Is

KahonKo Gadgets gives a limited warranty on every product sold through our website, kahonko.example, and the KahonKo app. The warranty promises that your product is free from **manufacturing defects** in materials and workmanship under normal use for the warranty period listed in Section 2. If a covered defect appears, we will repair or replace the product as described in these terms.

These Warranty Terms (Version 2.0) are effective **August 1, 2026** and apply to products delivered on or after that date. Products delivered earlier are covered by the terms in effect on their delivery date.

## 1.2 Warranty and Returns Are Different

The warranty and our **Return and Refund Policy (Version 3.0)** overlap in the first 15 days after delivery. It is important to know which one to use:

- **Days 1 to 7 after delivery:** a defective item is "Defective on Arrival" under the Return and Refund Policy. You may choose a **refund or a replacement**.
- **Days 8 to 15:** a defective item can be **replaced** under the Return and Refund Policy, but not refunded.
- **From day 16 until the end of the warranty period:** defects are handled under **these Warranty Terms**. We repair or replace the item. Cash refunds are given only in the special cases in Section 4.3.
- **Damage during delivery** (crushed parcel, cracked item on arrival) is **never** a warranty matter. It must be reported within **48 hours** under the Return and Refund Policy.

The warranty does not replace your rights under the Return and Refund Policy. When both apply, you may choose the one that is better for you.

## 1.3 Who Is Covered

The warranty covers the person who bought the product from KahonKo Gadgets and anyone who received the product **as a gift** from that person, as long as they can give the original order number (format KKO-########). The warranty is not transferable when a product is resold.

Products bought from resellers, online marketplaces, or any seller other than kahonko.example and the KahonKo app are **not** covered, because KahonKo Gadgets does not sell through those channels.

## 2.3 Phone Cases

For phone cases, the warranty covers defects such as buttons that stop working, peeling of the coating or leather, and clips or edges that crack **without** any impact. ## 2.4 Screen Protectors

The 30-day warranty for screen protectors covers only **lifting** or **bubbling** caused by a manufacturing defect, when the protector was installed as shown in the instructions using the included alignment frame. A screen protector that **breaks, cracks or chips is never covered**, because absorbing impact is its purpose.

# 3. What Is Covered and What Is Not

## 3.1 Covered

The warranty covers failures caused by a manufacturing defect under normal use, for example:

- a charger, cable or hub that stops charging or transferring data;
- a power bank that will not charge, will not hold a charge, or holds **less than 70%** of its rated capacity;
- a power bank that **swells** (see Section 5.3 for safety instructions);
- earbuds or headphones with no sound in one side, crackling, or failed pairing that does not go away after a reset;
- a speaker or wearable that will not turn on, charge or connect; and
- buttons, hinges or magnets that stop working without any impact.

## 3.2 Not Covered

| Not covered | Examples |
|---|---|
| Physical damage | Drops, cracks, dents, bent or broken connectors, crushed cables |
| Liquid damage | Except the rated water protection in Section 3.3 |
| Normal wear and tear | Scratches, fading, worn coating, frayed cable ends caused by bending at sharp angles |
| Opening or repair by others | Any sign that the product was opened, modified or repaired by someone other than KahonKo |
| Wrong power source | Damage from damaged, counterfeit or incompatible chargers, or from unstable power |
| Missing serial sticker | Serial number sticker removed, changed or unreadable |
| Lost or stolen items | — |
| Cosmetic issues that do not affect use | Small marks or color differences |
| Consumables | Ear tips, adhesive bases, remote control batteries (such as the CR2032 battery of the SnapPod remote) |
| Products not bought from KahonKo | Resellers and marketplaces |
| Damage during delivery | Must be claimed under the Return and Refund Policy within 48 hours |

## 3.3 Water Protection

Water protection depends on the product's rating, shown in the "water_rating" column of the Product Specs sheet:

| Rating | Products | Water damage covered? |
|---|---|---|
| **IP67** | BoomKahon Mini, BoomKahon Max | Yes, for water entry in fresh water up to 1 m for 30 minutes, with port covers closed |
| **5ATM** | FitBand 2, ActiveWatch S | Yes, for swimming in fresh water at the surface; not for diving, hot showers or sauna |
| **IPX5** | AirBuds Pro | Only rain, sweat and water jets. Submerging is not covered |
| **IPX4** | AirBuds Lite | Only splashes and sweat. Submerging is not covered |
| None | All other products | No |

Damage from **salt water, pool water, hot water, soap or steam** is never covered, whatever the rating. Water resistance can weaken over time, especially after drops.

## 3.4 Accidental Damage and KahonCare+

Accidental damage is not covered by the standard warranty. The only way to be covered for accidental damage is **KahonCare+** (Section 7), which covers **one** accidental damage claim.

# 4. What We Do When You Have a Covered Defect

## 4.1 Repair or Replacement

When a claim is approved, we will either:

- **repair** the product; or
- **replace** it with the same model, or with a model of equal or better specifications if the original is discontinued or out of stock.

We decide whether to repair or replace. Most accessories, such as cables and chargers, are replaced rather than repaired.

## 4.2 Warranty on the Replacement

A replacement or repaired product is covered for the **remaining** warranty period of the original product, or **90 days**, whichever is **longer**. For example, if your 6-month charger is replaced in month 5, the replacement is covered for 90 days, not 1 month.

## 4.3 When You Get Your Money Back

The warranty does not give cash refunds, except in these two cases:

1. **No replacement within 30 days.** If we cannot repair the product or ship a replacement within **30 days** after approving your claim, we refund the **purchase price**. You choose Store Credit or a refund to your original payment method.
2. **Three strikes.** If the **same item** fails with the **same defect three times** during its warranty period, we give a **full refund** of the purchase price on the third claim.

Refund timing is the same as in the Return and Refund Policy: Store Credit within 24 hours (with a 5% bonus, maximum ₱200), e-wallet in 3 to 5 business days, bank transfer in 5 to 7 business days, and card in 7 to 14 business days.

# 5. How to Make a Warranty Claim

## 5.1 What You Need

- Your **order number** (KKO-########) or the official receipt.
- The **serial number** of powered products (printed on the product or the box).
- A short description of the problem.
- Photos or a short **video** showing the defect.

## 5.2 Claim Steps

1. **Submit the claim** at kahonko.example/warranty or by email to warranty@kahonko.example.
2. **Troubleshoot with an agent.** A Customer Care agent may ask you to try another cable or adapter, fully charge the product, update the app, or reset Bluetooth pairing. About one in three claims is solved at this step.
3. **Get your WA number.** If the problem remains, we send a Warranty Authorization number (WA-######) within **2 business days**, together with a **prepaid shipping label**.
4. **Send the item.** Follow Section 6.
5. **Assessment.** Our Warranty Desk checks the item within **5 business days** after it arrives.
6. **Resolution.** We ship the repaired item or the replacement. Our target is **10 business days** from the day your item reaches us.

## 5.3 Swollen Power Banks: Do Not Ship

A swollen (puffed-up) power bank can be dangerous. **Stop using it immediately**, keep it away from heat and flammable items, and **do not ship it by courier**. Instead:

1. send clear photos of the swollen power bank, its serial number, and your order number to warranty@kahonko.example;
2. if it is within the 12-month warranty, we ship a replacement **without** asking for the old unit; and
3. bring the old unit to an electronic waste (e-waste) collection point near you.

## 5.4 No Fault Found

If our assessment finds that the item works normally and has no defect, we return it to you. A **₱150 handling fee** applies, and you pay the return shipping. To avoid this, please complete the troubleshooting steps first.

## 5.5 Claims Outside the Warranty Period

If your warranty has ended, we cannot repair or replace the item for free. As a thank-you to our customers, you may buy a new unit of the same product with **20% off**, once per product, by contacting Customer Care with the old order number.

# 7. KahonCare+ Extended Protection

## 7.1 What KahonCare+ Gives You

KahonCare+ is an optional plan you can add to eligible products:

- **12 extra months** of warranty after the standard warranty ends; and
- **one accidental damage claim** (such as a drop or liquid damage) during the whole coverage period, with a **₱200 service fee**.

## 7.2 Price and Eligibility

| Product price | KahonCare+ price |
|---|---|
| Below ₱1,000 | Not available |
| ₱1,000 to ₱2,999 | ₱149 |
| ₱3,000 and above | ₱299 |

For example, KahonCare+ costs ₱149 for the PocketBank 20000mAh (₱1,799) and ₱299 for the ActiveWatch S (₱3,999). It is not available for the PowerCube 20W charger (₱599). The KahonCare+ price of every product is listed in the "kahoncare_plus_php" column of the Product Specs sheet.

## 7.3 Rules

- KahonCare+ must be bought within **7 days** of the product's delivery, in **My Orders → Add KahonCare+**.
- It is linked to one product and one order number.
- After an accidental damage claim, the replaced product keeps the remaining KahonCare+ extended warranty, but **no further** accidental damage claims are allowed.
- Loss, theft and cosmetic damage are not covered.
- If you return the product under the Return and Refund Policy, KahonCare+ is cancelled and refunded in full. Otherwise, it cannot be refunded after 7 days.

## 7.4 Example

You buy the BoomKahon Max (₱3,499) with KahonCare+ (₱299). In month 15, you drop it and the speaker grille breaks. The standard 12-month warranty has ended and drops are never covered by the standard warranty. Because you have KahonCare+, you can make one accidental damage claim: you pay the ₱200 service fee and receive a replacement. The replacement is covered until the end of the KahonCare+ period (month 24), but a second accident would not be covered.

# 8. Comparing Returns and Warranty for Damaged Items

Damaged items are the most common source of confusion. Use this table:

| What happened | Return and Refund Policy | Warranty Terms |
|---|---|---|
| Item arrives cracked; reported within 48 hours with proof | Covered: full refund or replacement | Not applicable (delivery damage) |
| Item arrives cracked; reported after 48 hours | Not covered as transit damage | Not covered (physical damage) |
| Item has no visible damage but does not work on day 3 | Covered: refund or replacement (DOA) | Also covered, but only repair or replacement |
| Item stops working on day 10 | Covered: replacement only | Covered: repair or replacement |
| Power bank stops charging in month 9 | Not covered (after day 15) | Covered: 12-month warranty |
| Charger stops charging in month 9 | Not covered | Not covered (6-month warranty ended), unless KahonCare+ |
| Earbuds fall into the sea | Not covered | Not covered (salt water), unless KahonCare+ accident claim |
| Speaker dropped into a swimming pool | Not covered | Not covered (pool water), unless KahonCare+ accident claim |
| Final Sale cable is dead on arrival | Covered (DOA rules apply to Final Sale) | Covered |
