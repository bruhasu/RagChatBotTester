# 1. Overview

## 1.1 Our Promise

At KahonKo Gadgets we want every box you open to make you happy. If something is wrong with your order, or if you simply changed your mind, this policy explains what you can do, how long you have, who pays for shipping, and when you will get your money back.

KahonKo Gadgets is an **online-only** shop. We sell only through our website, kahonko.example, and the KahonKo app. We do not have a walk-in store, and we do not run official stores on online marketplaces. This policy applies only to orders placed on our website or app and delivered within the Philippines.

## 1.2 Scope and Related Documents

This Return and Refund Policy (Version 3.0) is effective **August 1, 2026** and replaces all earlier versions. It covers:

- returns because you changed your mind;
- items that arrive damaged, wrong or incomplete;
- items that are defective during the first 15 days; and
- refunds, exchanges and order cancellations.

Problems that appear **after the first 15 days** are handled under our **Warranty Terms (Version 2.0)**, not this policy. Delivery times, fees and courier questions are answered in our **Shipping FAQ**. Product details are in our **Product Specs** sheet.

# 2. Return Windows at a Glance

Every return window is counted in **calendar days from the delivery date**.

| Your situation | Report or request within | What you can get | Who pays shipping |
|---|---|---|---|
| Changed your mind | **15 days** | Refund of the item price, or an exchange | You (drop-off at a courier branch, or KahonKo pickup with ₱100 deducted) |
| Wrong item, missing item, or damaged in transit | **48 hours** | Full refund including shipping, or a replacement | KahonKo |
| Defective on arrival (DOA) | **Days 1 to 7** | Full refund including shipping, **or** a replacement | KahonKo |
| Defective | **Days 8 to 15** | **Replacement only** (no refund) | KahonKo |
| Defective | **After day 15** | Covered by the Warranty Terms (repair or replacement, no cash refund) | See Warranty Terms |
| Damaged by you (dropped, wet, misused) | — | Not returnable | — |

> Example: Your PowerCube 20W charger was delivered on September 1. You can return it for change of mind until September 16. If it stops working on September 6, that is DOA: you may choose a refund or a replacement. If it stops working on September 12, you can get a replacement but not a refund. If it stops working in November, it is a warranty claim.

# 3. Change-of-Mind Returns

## 3.1 Conditions

You may return an item within **15 calendar days** of delivery if you simply changed your mind, as long as **all** of these are true:

- the item is **unused**, clean and in the same condition as when you received it;
- it is in its **original box**, with all inner packaging, accessories, cables, manuals and stickers;
- any **free gift** that came with it is also returned (or its listed value is deducted from your refund); and
- the item is not in the list of non-returnable items in Section 3.2.

## 3.2 Items You Cannot Return for Change of Mind

| Item | Why | Exception |
|---|---|---|
| Items marked **Final Sale** (for example, the Basic 12W USB-A Charger and the 3-in-1 Charging Cable) | Clearance pricing | DOA, transit damage and warranty rights still apply |
| **Custom or engraved** items (for example, the Custom Engraved Leather Case) | Made to order for you | DOA, transit damage and warranty rights still apply |
| **Earbuds and in-ear earphones** with a broken hygiene seal | Hygiene | Returnable if the seal is unbroken; defective items always accepted |
| **Screen protectors** in an opened pack | Hygiene and fit | Returnable if the pack is unopened |
| Gift cards | — | None |

Over-ear headphones (such as the WaveMax Over-Ear Headphones) **can** be returned for change of mind if they are unused, because they do not go inside the ear.

## 3.3 Shipping Costs for Change-of-Mind Returns

- The **original shipping fee is not refunded**.
- You choose how to send the item back:
  - **Drop-off:** bring the parcel to any LakbayExpress branch and pay the courier yourself; or
  - **KahonKo pickup:** we send a courier to your address and deduct **₱100** from your refund.
- If your order had free shipping, we do not charge you for the original shipping when you return an item.

## 3.4 Returns That Fail Inspection

If a change-of-mind return arrives used, damaged, incomplete or without its original box, we cannot accept it. We will send it back to you, and you pay **₱100** for the return shipping. You will not receive a refund for that item.

## 3.5 Worked Example

You bought AirBuds Lite (₱1,299) and a FlexWeave USB-C cable (₱349) with ₱79 Metro Manila shipping. You open the earbuds, try them once, and decide you do not like them. Because the hygiene seal is broken, the earbuds cannot be returned for change of mind. The cable is unopened, so you may return it within 15 days and receive ₱349, or ₱249 if you choose KahonKo pickup. The ₱79 shipping fee is not refunded.

# 4. Wrong, Missing or Damaged-in-Transit Items

## 4.1 Report Within 48 Hours

If your parcel arrives with the wrong item, a missing item, or an item damaged during delivery, report it within **48 hours** of the delivery time shown in tracking. Use **My Orders → Request Return** and choose the matching reason, or email returns@kahonko.example with your order number (format KKO-########).

## 4.2 Proof We Need

| Problem | Proof required |
|---|---|
| **Missing item** or **wrong item** | A continuous **unboxing video**. The video must start with the **sealed** parcel, show the shipping label clearly, and continue without cuts until all contents are shown. |
| **Damaged in transit** | An unboxing video, **or** clear photos of the damaged outer box taken on the delivery day, plus photos of the damaged item. |

Without an unboxing video, we cannot approve a missing-item or wrong-item claim. We strongly recommend recording a video every time you open a KahonKo parcel.

## 4.3 What You Get

After we approve your claim, you may choose:

- a **replacement** of the same item, shipped free; or
- a **full refund** of the item price **and** the shipping fee you paid.

KahonKo pays all shipping costs. For damaged or wrong items, we arrange a free pickup. For missing items, nothing needs to be returned.

## 4.4 Refusing a Damaged Parcel at the Door

If the outer box is visibly crushed, torn or wet when the courier arrives, you may **refuse to accept** the parcel. Tell the courier the reason. Once the parcel is back at our warehouse, we refund your payment in full (or cancel your COD order at no charge). Refusing a visibly damaged parcel does **not** count as a refused COD delivery.

# 5. Defective Items in the First 15 Days

## 5.1 Days 1 to 7: Defective on Arrival (DOA)

If an item does not work, or stops working, within **7 calendar days** of delivery because of a manufacturing defect, it is **Defective on Arrival**. You may choose:

- a **full refund**, including the shipping fee you paid; or
- a **free replacement** of the same item.

KahonKo pays the shipping both ways. The original box is helpful but **not required** for defective items.

## 5.2 Days 8 to 15: Replacement Only

If the defect appears from **day 8 to day 15**, we will send a **replacement** of the same item (or an equal item if it is out of stock). **Refunds are not available** for defects found in this period, except when no replacement can be supplied within 30 days. KahonKo pays the shipping both ways.

## 5.3 After Day 15

From day 16, a defective item is handled under the **Warranty Terms**. Warranty periods depend on the product category. For example, chargers and cables have a **6-month** warranty and power banks have a **12-month** warranty. Under the warranty, we repair or replace the item. Cash refunds are given only in the special cases listed in the Warranty Terms.

## 5.4 Troubleshooting First

Before approving a defect claim, a Customer Care agent may ask you to try simple checks, such as using a different cable or wall adapter, charging the item for 2 hours, or resetting Bluetooth pairing. Many "defects" are caused by a faulty cable or an adapter that is too weak. For example, the MagPad 15W and the Trio 3-in-1 Wireless Stand need a USB-C adapter of **20W or higher**, which is not included in the box.

## 5.5 Damage You Caused

Items damaged after delivery by drops, cracks, liquid, bending, heat, or use with incompatible chargers are **not** returnable and are **not** covered by the warranty. The only exception is an item protected by **KahonCare+**, which covers one accidental damage claim. See the Warranty Terms.

## 5.6 Final Sale and Custom Items

Final Sale and custom items cannot be returned for change of mind, but they are treated like any other item when they are **defective**, **wrong**, **missing** or **damaged in transit**. A Final Sale charger that is dead on day 3 can be refunded or replaced.

# 6. How to Return an Item

## 6.1 Step by Step

1. **Request the return.** In the KahonKo app or at kahonko.example, go to **My Orders → Request Return**. You can also email returns@kahonko.example with your order number.
2. **Choose the reason and upload proof.** Add photos or the unboxing video when required.
3. **Get your RA number.** We review your request and send a Return Authorization number (RA-######) within **1 business day**.
4. **Pack the item.** Use the original box for change-of-mind returns. Write the RA number clearly on the outside of the package.
5. **Send it within 5 calendar days.** Drop it off at a LakbayExpress branch or wait for the KahonKo pickup. If you do not send it within **5 calendar days** after the RA is approved, the RA expires and you must request a new one (still within your return window).
6. **Inspection.** Our Returns Center inspects the item within **3 business days** after it arrives.
7. **Refund or replacement.** After approval, we start your refund or ship your replacement.

# 7. Refunds

## 7.1 Refund Methods and Timing

Refunds start after your return is approved. The time to receive your money depends on the method:

| Refund method | Time after approval |
|---|---|
| **KahonKo Store Credit** | Within **24 hours**, plus a **5% bonus** (maximum ₱200) |
| E-wallet | 3 to 5 business days |
| Online bank transfer | 5 to 7 business days |
| Credit or debit card | 7 to 14 business days (depends on your bank) |

By default, we refund to your original payment method. For **Cash on Delivery** orders, we refund by **online bank transfer** or e-wallet, or as Store Credit if you prefer. Store Credit is valid for **12 months** and cannot be converted to cash.

## 7.2 How Much You Get Back

| Situation | Refund amount |
|---|---|
| Change of mind | Item price you paid. Original shipping not refunded. ₱100 deducted if you used KahonKo pickup. |
| DOA (days 1 to 7), refund chosen | Item price plus the shipping fee you paid |
| Wrong, missing or damaged in transit, refund chosen | Item price plus the shipping fee you paid |
| Order cancelled before dispatch | Everything you paid, including shipping |
| Delivery refused after dispatch (prepaid order, not damaged) | Item price minus the original shipping fee |
| Parcel declared lost | Everything you paid, or a free replacement |

## 7.3 Vouchers and Discounts

- You get back the amount you **actually paid**. Vouchers are never paid out as cash.
- If you return the **whole order**, a voucher you used is returned to your account if it has not expired.
- If you return **part of an order**, the refund for each returned item is its share of the amount you paid after the voucher. For example, if a ₱200 voucher was used on a ₱2,000 order, each item is refunded at 90% of its listed price.

## 7.4 Bundles and Free Gifts

- Bundles must be returned complete for a full refund. If you return one item from a bundle, it is refunded at its share of the bundle price.
- Free gifts must be returned with the main item. If a free gift is missing, its listed value on your order is deducted from your refund.

## 7.5 Sale Items

Items bought during the **11.11 Sale (November 11, 2026)**, **Black Friday (November 27, 2026)** and the **12.12 Sale (December 12, 2026)** follow the normal rules in this policy, unless they are marked **Final Sale**. We do not offer price matching or price adjustments if an item goes on sale after you buy it.

## 7.6 Extended Holiday Returns

Items delivered from **November 15 to December 24, 2026** can be returned for change of mind until **January 8, 2027**, even if the normal 15-day window has passed. All other conditions still apply. The extension does not apply to the 48-hour and 7-day windows for damaged, wrong, missing or defective items.

# 8. Exchanges

You may exchange an item for a different color or a different model within **15 calendar days** of delivery.

- The item must meet all change-of-mind conditions in Section 3.1.
- Each item can be exchanged **only once**.
- If the new item costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, we refund the difference to your original payment method or as Store Credit.
- Shipping follows the change-of-mind rules: drop-off, or KahonKo pickup for ₱100. We ship the new item for free.
- Non-returnable items (Section 3.2) cannot be exchanged, except for defects.

# 9. Cancelling an Order

## 9.1 Before Dispatch

You can cancel any order **before it is dispatched** at no charge, in **My Orders → Cancel Order**, or by contacting Customer Care. You receive a full refund, including shipping. Orders paid by 2:00 PM from Monday to Saturday are usually dispatched the same business day, so please cancel quickly.

## 9.2 After Dispatch

After dispatch, we cannot cancel the order or change the address. You may:

- **refuse the delivery**: for prepaid orders, we refund the item price minus the original shipping fee once the parcel is back at our warehouse; for COD orders, nothing is charged, but refusing COD parcels **two times** turns off COD on your account; or
- **accept the parcel and request a change-of-mind return** within 15 days.

## 9.3 Pre-Orders

Pre-order items (such as the ActiveWatch S, shipping from November 20, 2026) can be cancelled any time before dispatch for a full refund.

# 10. KahonCare+ and Returns

KahonCare+ is our optional extended protection plan. It is described fully in the Warranty Terms. For returns:

- KahonCare+ must be bought within **7 days** of delivery.
- If you return the item it protects, KahonCare+ is **cancelled and fully refunded**.
- KahonCare+ cannot be returned on its own after 7 days.
