# 1. General Information

## 1.1 Purpose

Bagwis University believes that no qualified Bagwisian should stop studying because of cost. This policy describes the scholarships, grants, discounts, emergency assistance and payment arrangements available to undergraduate students in Academic Year (AY) 2026–2027. It explains who can apply, what each program covers, how to keep it, and what happens when a student no longer qualifies.

This policy works together with two other university documents. The **Grading and Academic Policy** defines the General Weighted Average (GWA), incomplete grades (INC) and academic standing (Warning, Probation, Dismissal). The **Enrollment and Registration Policy** defines unit loads, payment plans and refunds. Many questions about scholarships can only be answered by reading the rules of this policy together with those documents.

## 1.3 Grading Scale Reminder

Bagwis University uses a scale where **4.0 is the highest grade, 1.0 is the lowest passing grade, and 0.0 is a failing grade**. A higher GWA is better. All GWA requirements in this policy use the recorded GWA, which is truncated to two decimal places and never rounded up. A recorded GWA of 3.49 does not meet a 3.50 requirement.

## 2.1 Application Periods for AY 2026–2027

| Second semester | October 5 to 30, 2026 | November 10, 2026 |

## 2.2 How to Apply

1. Log in to BagwisNet and open the "Scholarships" tab.
2. Choose the program and fill in the online application form.
3. Upload or submit the required documents (Section 2.3) to the OSFA, Kalapati Hall Room 204.
4. Attend the interview if the program requires one (the Bagwis Tulong Grant and the Student Assistantship Program require an interview).
5. Wait for the results on BagwisNet and on the OSFA bulletin board.

## 2.3 Common Documents

## 2.4 Enrolling While the Result Is Pending

A student whose application is still being processed may enroll by paying the down payment of ₱5,000. If the scholarship is approved, the benefit is applied to the student's account and any excess payment is credited to the next installment or refunded at the end of the semester.

# 3. Bagwis Academic Excellence Scholarship (BAES)

## 3.1 Benefits

| Level | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Full scholar | 100% of tuition plus a book allowance of ₱2,000 per semester |
| Partial scholar | 50% of tuition |

## 3.2 Requirements for Incoming Freshmen

| Level | Senior High School general average | Other requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Full | 95 or higher | Passed the BUCAT |
| Partial | 92 to 94 | Passed the BUCAT |

## 3.3 Requirements for Continuing Students

A continuing student qualifies for the BAES based on the grades of the **previous regular semester**:

| Level | Semester GWA |
|---|---|
| Full | 3.75 or higher |
| Partial | 3.50 to 3.74 |

In addition, **all** of these conditions must be met:

- **no grade lower than 2.5** in any course in the previous semester;
- at least **18 units** enrolled in the previous semester and in the current semester (a graduating student with fewer than 18 units left in the curriculum is exempt);
- **no unresolved INC** by the scholarship renewal deadline;
- **not** on Academic Warning or Academic Probation; and
- no major disciplinary offense.

## 3.4 Upgrades and Downgrades

The BAES level is checked every semester:

- A full scholar whose semester GWA is 3.50 to 3.74 is **downgraded** to partial for the next semester.
- A partial scholar whose semester GWA is 3.75 or higher is **upgraded** to full for the next semester.
- A scholar whose semester GWA falls below 3.50, or who gets any grade lower than 2.5, **loses** the BAES for the next semester. The student may apply again after one semester with qualifying grades.

## 3.5 BAES and the Dean's List

The BAES and the Dean's List use the same GWA threshold (3.50) and the same lowest grade (2.5). However, the Dean's List needs only 15 units, while the BAES needs at least 18 units. A student on the Dean's List with only 15 or 16 units does **not** qualify for the BAES.

# 4. Need-Based and Work-Study Programs

## 4.1 Bagwis Tulong Grant

The Bagwis Tulong Grant helps students from low-income families.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Benefit | 75% of tuition |
| Family income | Annual family income of ₱300,000 or less |
| Grades | GWA of 2.50 or higher and no grade of 0.0 in the previous semester |
| Load | At least 15 units |
| Service | 40 hours of community service per semester, arranged through the Office of Student Affairs |
| Interview | Required |
| Slots | 150 grants per semester |

When there are more qualified applicants than slots, the Scholarship Committee gives priority to the applicants with the lowest family income, then to those with the higher GWA.

## 4.2 Student Assistantship Program (SAP)

Student assistants work in university offices such as the Library, the Registrar and the ICT Services Office.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Benefit | 100% of tuition for up to 21 units, plus a monthly allowance of ₱2,000 |
| Grades | GWA of 2.00 or higher and no grade of 0.0 |
| Maximum load | 21 units (a student assistant may not enroll in more than 21 units) |
| Work | 15 hours per week in an assigned office |
| Interview | Required |

A student assistant who misses more than three scheduled work shifts in a month without a valid reason receives a written warning. A second warning ends the assistantship.

# 6. Discounts

## 6.1 Sibling Discount

When two or more siblings are enrolled at Bagwis University in the same semester, the **younger** siblings receive a discount on tuition:

| Sibling | Discount |
|---|---|
| Eldest enrolled sibling | None |
| Second enrolled sibling | 10% of tuition |
| Third and later siblings | 15% of tuition each |

## 6.2 Full-Payment Discount

Students who pay all fees in full by the last day of regular enrollment receive a **5% discount on tuition** (see the Enrollment and Registration Policy). For scholars, this discount is computed on the tuition that is **left after** the scholarship, grant or sibling discount is deducted.

# 7. Combining Benefits (Stacking Rules)

## 7.1 One Scholarship or Grant at a Time

A student may hold **only one** university scholarship or grant in a semester. A student who qualifies for two programs keeps the one with the **higher benefit**. For example, a student who qualifies for both the Performing Arts Grant (50%) and the Bagwis Tulong Grant (75%) receives only the Tulong Grant.

## 7.2 The Sibling Discount

The sibling discount can be combined **only with the Bagwis Tulong Grant**. It cannot be combined with the BAES, the SAP, the Agila Varsity Scholarship or the Performing Arts Grant. When it is combined with the Tulong Grant, the sibling discount is computed on the tuition left after the grant.

## 7.3 External Scholarships

A student with a scholarship from an outside sponsor (for example, a local government or a private foundation) must register it with the OSFA. An external scholarship may be combined with a university scholarship as long as the total benefit does **not exceed 100% of tuition**. If the total would exceed 100%, the university scholarship is reduced.

## 7.4 Worked Examples

**Example 1: Tulong Grant + sibling discount + full payment.** A second sibling takes 20 units. Tuition is 20 × ₱1,450 = ₱29,000. The Tulong Grant covers 75%, leaving ₱7,250. The 10% sibling discount on the remaining tuition is ₱725, leaving ₱6,525. If the student pays in full by the last day of regular enrollment, the 5% full-payment discount is ₱326.25, so tuition due is ₱6,198.75. The miscellaneous fee of ₱6,500 is still payable in full.

**Example 2: Partial BAES + sibling discount.** A partial BAES scholar is also a second sibling. The sibling discount cannot be combined with the BAES, so the student receives only the 50% BAES benefit.

**Example 3: Full BAES + external scholarship.** A full BAES scholar also receives an outside scholarship covering 30% of tuition. Since the BAES already covers 100% of tuition, the total would exceed 100%. The student must choose: keep the BAES (and the book allowance), or reduce the BAES so that the total stays at 100% of tuition.

# 8. Keeping a Scholarship: Renewal Rules

## 8.1 Renewal Every Semester

All scholarships and grants are reviewed every semester after grades are posted. Renewal documents are due **two weeks after grades are posted**:

| Grades of | Grades posted | Renewal deadline |
|---|---|---|
| First semester AY 2026–2027 | October 31, 2026 | November 13, 2026 |
| Second semester AY 2026–2027 | March 27, 2027 | April 9, 2027 |

## 8.2 Incomplete Grades (INC)

An INC puts the scholarship renewal **on hold**. The rules depend on what happens to the INC:

- If the INC is completed **by the renewal deadline** and the grades still meet the program's requirements, the scholarship is renewed.
- If the INC is **not** completed by the renewal deadline, the scholarship is **lost for the next semester**, even if the student completes the INC later within the INC deadline of the Grading and Academic Policy.
- If the INC becomes 0.0, the scholarship is lost, because almost every program requires no grade of 0.0.

Example: A BAES scholar gets an INC in the first semester of AY 2026–2027. The Grading and Academic Policy allows the student until March 20, 2027 to complete the INC. However, to keep the scholarship for the second semester, the student must complete it by the scholarship renewal deadline of **November 13, 2026**.

## 8.3 Academic Standing

Academic standing is decided under the Grading and Academic Policy. Its effect on scholarships is:

| Standing | Effect on scholarships and grants |
|---|---|
| Good standing | Normal renewal rules apply |
| Academic Warning | BAES is lost. Other programs continue only if their own GWA rules are met. |
| Academic Probation | **All** university scholarships and grants are **suspended** for the probation semester (BAES, Tulong Grant, SAP, Agila Varsity Scholarship and Performing Arts Grant). |
| Dismissal | Scholarship is cancelled |

A student whose probation is lifted may apply again in the next application period. Suspension is not automatic reinstatement: the student must reapply and meet the requirements again.

## 8.4 Unit Load

A BAES scholar must enroll in at least 18 units, and a Tulong Grant holder in at least 15 units. Note that a student on Academic Warning may take at most 18 units and a student on probation at most 15 units, under the Enrollment and Registration Policy.

## 8.5 Discipline

A scholar found guilty of a major offense under the Student Handbook loses the scholarship starting from the semester after the decision. A scholar found guilty of academic dishonesty loses it immediately.

# 9. Withdrawal, Leave of Absence and Refunds

## 9.1 Withdrawing from the University

If a scholar officially withdraws from the university during a semester, the scholarship is **cancelled**. The refund schedule of the Enrollment and Registration Policy applies only to the amount the student personally paid. Amounts covered by a scholarship or grant are **never refunded to the student**. They are returned to the scholarship fund.

Example: A Tulong Grant holder paid ₱7,250 of tuition (the other 75% was covered by the grant) and withdraws within the first week of classes. The student gets 75% of the ₱7,250 personally paid, which is ₱5,437.50. The grant portion goes back to the fund.

## 9.2 Leave of Absence

A scholar who goes on an approved Leave of Absence may keep the scholarship upon return, if the leave is not longer than one semester and the student met all requirements in the last semester attended. A leave of two semesters requires a new application.

# 10. Emergency Assistance

## 10.1 Emergency Financial Assistance

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Benefit | One-time grant of up to ₱10,000 per academic year |
| Who may apply | Students affected by a calamity (such as a typhoon or flood), a fire, or the death of the family breadwinner |
| Deadline | Within 30 calendar days after the event |
| Documents | Barangay certification of the event; death certificate if applicable |

This assistance does not count as a scholarship, so it can be received together with any scholarship or grant.

## 10.2 Emergency Student Loan

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Amount | Up to ₱15,000, interest-free |
| Repayment | Before the final examinations of the same semester |
| Co-signer | A parent or guardian must co-sign |
| Limit | A student may not have more than one outstanding loan |

## 10.3 Promissory Notes

A student who cannot pay an installment before an examination may ask the Accounting Office, Agila Hall Room 105, for a promissory note. A promissory note allows the student to take the midterm or final examinations with an unpaid balance. A student may use at most **two promissory notes per semester**, and the balance must be paid within **30 days** or before the next examination, whichever comes first. Unpaid balances block enrollment for the next term and the release of the Transcript of Records.

# 11. Appeals

A student who loses or is denied a scholarship may appeal to the Scholarship Committee within **5 working days** after receiving the notice. Appeals are accepted only for documented medical or family emergencies that affected the student's grades or ability to meet a requirement. Each student may be granted only **one** appeal per scholarship program during the student's whole stay at Bagwis University. The Committee's decision is final.

# 12. Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can apply for an academic scholarship?

Continuing students with a semester GWA of 3.50 or higher, no grade lower than 2.5, at least 18 units, and good academic standing. A GWA of 3.75 or higher gives a full scholarship; 3.50 to 3.74 gives a partial scholarship.

### Does my scholarship pay for the miscellaneous fee?

No, except the Agila Varsity Scholarship. All other programs cover tuition only.

### Can I have two scholarships?

No. You can hold only one university scholarship or grant per semester. The only discount you can add is the sibling discount, and only together with the Bagwis Tulong Grant.

### I was placed on probation. What happens to my grant?

All university scholarships and grants are suspended for the probation semester. After probation is lifted, you must apply again.

### When is the deadline to apply for the second semester?

Applications for the second semester are accepted from October 5 to 30, 2026. Results are posted on November 10, 2026.
