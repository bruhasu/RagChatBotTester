# 1. General Provisions

## 1.1 Purpose and Scope

This Grading and Academic Policy sets the rules for grading, computing the General Weighted Average (GWA), incomplete grades, attendance, academic standing, academic honors and grade appeals at Bagwis University. It applies to all undergraduate students in all colleges, starting with the first semester of Academic Year (AY) 2026–2027, which begins on June 29, 2026. The policy was approved by the Academic Council on March 2, 2026 and is administered by the Office of the Vice President for Academic Affairs (OVPAA), headed by Dr. Fernando G. Ocampo.

Many of the rules in this policy depend on each other. For example, a student's GWA decides the student's academic standing; academic standing decides the maximum unit load and whether scholarships continue; and having ever been on probation decides whether the student can graduate with Latin honors. Students are encouraged to read the whole policy, not only one section.

## 1.2 Key Principle: Higher Number Means Better

Bagwis University uses a **4-point grading scale where a higher number is better**. The highest grade is **4.0**. The lowest passing grade is **1.0**. A failing grade is **0.0**. Students who come from schools that use a 1.0-is-highest system should take note: at Bagwis, a 1.0 is a pass but it is the lowest pass, not the best grade.

# 2. The Grading System

## 2.1 Grade Scale

| Grade | Percentage | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4.0 | 96–100 | Excellent (highest grade) |
| 3.5 | 91–95 | Superior |
| 3.0 | 86–90 | Very Good |
| 2.5 | 81–85 | Good |
| 2.0 | 76–80 | Satisfactory |
| 1.5 | 71–75 | Fair |
| 1.0 | 65–70 | Passed (lowest passing grade) |
| 0.0 | Below 65 | Failed |

The passing percentage is **65**. A student who gets a final percentage of 64 receives 0.0.

## 2.2 Other Grade Marks

| Mark | Meaning | Counted in GWA? |
|---|---|---|
| INC | Incomplete. Class standing is passing but a requirement or the final exam is missing for a valid reason. | Not counted until completed. If it becomes 0.0, it is counted. |
| W | Officially withdrawn from the course before the withdrawal deadline. | No |
| FA | Failure due to absences (absences above the 20% limit). | Yes, as 0.0 |

## 2.3 How a Final Grade Is Computed

Each semester has two grading terms: the midterm and the final term.

- **Term grade** = 60% class standing (quizzes, recitations, projects, assignments, laboratory work) + 40% term examination.
- **Final grade** = **40% midterm grade + 60% final term grade.**
- For courses with a laboratory component, the course grade is 60% lecture and 40% laboratory.

The final percentage is rounded to the nearest whole number **before** it is converted to the 4-point scale. For example, a final percentage of 85.5 is rounded to 86, which converts to 3.0. A final percentage of 85.4 is rounded to 85, which converts to 2.5.

### Worked example

A student has a midterm grade of 82% and a final term grade of 90%. The final percentage is (0.40 × 82) + (0.60 × 90) = 32.8 + 54.0 = 86.8, which rounds to 87, so the final grade is **3.0**.

## 2.4 Posting of Grades

Faculty submit final grades to the Registrar after the final examinations. Grades are posted on BagwisNet on **October 31, 2026** for the first semester and on **March 27, 2027** for the second semester. A student with an unpaid balance can see grades on BagwisNet but cannot download a Certificate of Grades until the balance is paid.

# 3. General Weighted Average (GWA)

## 3.1 Formula

The GWA is the average of a student's grades, weighted by the number of units of each course:

**GWA = (sum of grade × units for each course) ÷ (sum of units)**

The **semester GWA** uses only the courses of one semester. The **cumulative GWA** uses all courses taken at Bagwis University, including the midyear term.

## 3.2 What Is Included and Excluded

| Included in GWA | Excluded from GWA |
|---|---|
| All graded academic courses | NSTP (National Service Training Program) courses |
| Physical Education (PE) courses | Courses with W |
| Grades of 0.0 | INC grades that have not yet been completed |
| FA (counted as 0.0) | Credited courses from another school (transferees) |
| Repeated courses: both the old and new grade | |

## 3.3 Truncation, Not Rounding

The GWA is computed to four decimal places and then **truncated** to two decimal places. It is **never rounded up**. For example, a computed GWA of 3.4983 is recorded as **3.49**, not 3.50. This matters because many thresholds in this policy and in the Scholarships and Financial Aid Policy use exact values such as 3.50, 2.00 and 3.75. A recorded GWA of 3.49 does **not** meet a 3.50 requirement.

## 3.4 Worked Example

| Course | Units | Grade | Grade × units |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mathematics in the Modern World | 3 | 3.5 | 10.5 |
| Purposive Communication | 3 | 4.0 | 12.0 |
| Readings in Philippine History | 3 | 3.0 | 9.0 |
| Introduction to Computing (with lab) | 3 | 3.5 | 10.5 |
| Science, Technology and Society | 3 | 3.0 | 9.0 |
| Physical Education 1 | 2 | 4.0 | 8.0 |
| NSTP 1 | 3 | 3.0 | excluded |
| **Total (excluding NSTP)** | **17** | | **59.0** |

Semester GWA = 59.0 ÷ 17 = 3.4705..., recorded as **3.47**. This student does not reach 3.50, so the student is not on the Dean's List and does not qualify for the Bagwis Academic Excellence Scholarship for the next semester, even though most grades are 3.0 or higher.

## 3.5 Repeating a Course

A student who fails a course must take it again. Both the failing grade and the new grade stay on the Transcript of Records, and both count in the cumulative GWA. A student may take the same course at most three times. Failing the same course **three times** results in dismissal from the program (see Section 6).

# 4. Incomplete Grades (INC)

## 4.1 When an INC Is Given

An instructor gives an INC only when **both** of these are true:

- the student's class standing is passing; and
- the student missed the final examination or a major requirement for a valid reason, such as illness (with a medical certificate) or a family emergency.

A student who is failing the class standing receives 0.0, not INC, even if the final exam was missed.

## 4.2 Deadline to Complete an INC

An INC must be completed within **one semester**, which means by the **last day of the final examinations of the next regular semester**. The midyear term does not count as a semester for this rule.

| INC received in | Completion deadline |
|---|---|
| Second semester, AY 2025–2026 | October 24, 2026 |
| First semester, AY 2026–2027 | March 20, 2027 |
| Second semester, AY 2026–2027 | Last day of first-semester final exams, AY 2027–2028 |
| Midyear term 2027 | Same as second semester AY 2026–2027 INCs |

## 4.3 What Happens if the INC Is Not Completed

An INC that is not completed by the deadline **automatically becomes 0.0**. No action from the instructor is needed. The 0.0 is then counted in the GWA of the semester in which the INC was originally given, and the student's academic standing for that semester is recomputed.

## 4.4 How to Complete an INC

1. Get an INC completion form from the Registrar, Agila Hall Room 101.
2. Pay the INC completion fee of **₱150 per course** at the Cashier, Agila Hall Room 103.
3. Bring the form and the official receipt to the instructor and complete the missing requirement or exam.
4. The instructor submits the completed grade to the Registrar.

## 4.5 Effects of an INC on Other Rules

- **Dean's List:** A student with any INC in a semester is **not eligible** for the Dean's List or President's List for that semester, even if the INC is completed later with a high grade.
- **Scholarships:** Under the Scholarships and Financial Aid Policy, an INC puts scholarship renewal **on hold**. It must be completed by the scholarship renewal deadline, or the scholarship is lost for the next semester.
- **Graduation:** Graduating students must have **no INC** on record by **March 20, 2027**.
- **Latin honors:** An INC that became 0.0 counts as a grade below 2.0, which removes eligibility for Latin honors.

# 5. Attendance

## 5.1 Absence Limit

A student who is absent for more than **20%** of the total class hours of a course may be given a grade of **FA** (failure due to absences), which counts as 0.0. For a 3-unit course with 54 class hours, 20% is 10.8 hours, so the student may be given FA after being absent for **11 hours** or more.

## 5.2 Tardiness

- A student who arrives more than **15 minutes** after the class starts is marked **late**.
- A student who arrives more than **30 minutes** after the class starts is marked **absent**.
- **Three lates count as one absence.**

## 5.3 Excused Absences

Absences with an excuse slip from the University Clinic or the Office of Student Affairs are still counted toward the 20% limit, but the instructor must allow the student to make up missed quizzes and exams. The Clinic issues a medical certificate only if the student was seen at the Clinic, or if the student presents an outside doctor's certificate within **3 school days** after returning to class.

## 5.4 Official University Activities

Students who represent the university in official activities (for example, varsity games of the Bagwis Agilas or competitions) are not marked absent if they present an approved excuse letter from the Office of Student Affairs before the activity.

# 6. Academic Standing

## 6.1 When Standing Is Decided

Academic standing is decided by the Registrar after grades are posted at the end of each **regular** semester. The midyear term does **not** change a student's standing, but midyear grades are included in the cumulative GWA.

"Failed units" means the units of courses with 0.0 or FA. "Enrolled units" means all units the student was enrolled in that semester, excluding NSTP and courses with W.

## 6.2 Standing Levels

| Standing | When it applies |
|---|---|
| Good standing | None of the conditions below apply. |
| **Academic Warning** | Failed units are 25% to less than 50% of enrolled units, **or** the semester GWA is below 2.00. |
| **Academic Probation** | Failed units are 50% to less than 75% of enrolled units, **or** the student receives a second Academic Warning in a row (two consecutive regular semesters). |
| **Dismissal from the program** | Failed units are 75% or more of enrolled units, **or** the student does not lift probation in the next regular semester, **or** the student fails the same course three times. |
| **Debarment from the university** | The student is dismissed from a program for a second time. |

## 6.3 Effects of Academic Warning

- Maximum load of **18 units** in the next semester.
- The student must meet the academic adviser before enrolling and once during the semester.
- The student is not on the Dean's List (the GWA is below 3.50 or failures exist).
- Under the Scholarships and Financial Aid Policy, a student on Academic Warning cannot keep the Bagwis Academic Excellence Scholarship. Other grants have their own GWA rules.

## 6.4 Effects of Academic Probation

A student on Academic Probation:

- may take at most **15 units**;
- must sign a probation contract with the dean;
- must meet the academic adviser **every month**;
- must attend at least one counseling session at the Guidance and Counseling Center;
- cannot hold an officer position in any student organization and cannot run for the Bagwis Supreme Student Council;
- cannot take an overload and cannot cross-enroll at another school;
- has **all university scholarships and grants suspended** for the probation semester. This includes the Bagwis Academic Excellence Scholarship, the Bagwis Tulong Grant, the Student Assistantship Program, the Agila Varsity Scholarship and the Performing Arts Grant; and
- becomes permanently **ineligible for Latin honors** (see Section 7.3).

## 6.5 Lifting Probation

Probation is lifted if, in the next regular semester, the student gets a semester GWA of **2.00 or higher** and **no 0.0 or FA** grade. The student then returns to good standing. If probation is not lifted, the student is dismissed from the program.

## 6.6 Dismissal and Debarment

A student dismissed from a program may apply **once** to shift to a different program in the university. The dean of the receiving college decides on the application. A student who is dismissed from a program a second time is debarred and may not enroll again at Bagwis University.

## 6.7 Worked Examples

**Example A.** A student enrolled in 21 units fails one 3-unit course. Failed units = 3 ÷ 21 = 14%. The semester GWA is 2.40. The student remains in **good standing**, because failures are below 25% and the GWA is not below 2.00.

**Example B.** A student enrolled in 18 units fails two 3-unit courses (6 units = 33%). The student is placed on **Academic Warning** and may take only 18 units next semester.

**Example C.** The same student in Example B gets a semester GWA of 1.85 in the next semester with no failures. Because the GWA is below 2.00, the student receives a second consecutive warning, which becomes **Academic Probation**. Any university scholarship or grant the student still holds is suspended, the maximum load becomes 15 units, and the student can no longer receive Latin honors.

**Example D.** A student with an INC in a 3-unit course does not complete it by the deadline. The INC becomes 0.0 and the standing for that semester is recomputed. If the new failed units reach 25%, the student is placed on Academic Warning retroactively, which may affect the student's scholarship for the current semester.

# 7. Academic Honors

## 7.1 Dean's List

A student is included in the Dean's List for a semester if **all** of these are true:

- semester GWA of **3.50 or higher**;
- at least **15 units** enrolled, excluding NSTP;
- **no grade below 2.5** in any course;
- **no INC, W, 0.0 or FA** in the semester; and
- no disciplinary sanction during the semester.

## 7.2 President's List

A student who meets all the Dean's List conditions **and** has a semester GWA of **3.75 or higher** is included in the President's List instead of the Dean's List. Names are posted on the college bulletin boards and on BagwisNet about two weeks after grades are posted.

## 7.3 Latin Honors at Graduation

| Honor | Cumulative GWA (excluding NSTP) |
|---|---|
| Summa cum laude | 3.80 or higher |
| Magna cum laude | 3.60 to 3.79 |
| Cum laude | 3.40 to 3.59 |

In addition to the GWA, a candidate for Latin honors must meet **all** of these conditions:

- no final grade below **2.0** in any course taken at Bagwis, including an INC that became 0.0;
- **never** placed on Academic Probation;
- at least **75%** of the program's units taken at Bagwis University (this mainly affects transferees);
- finished the program within its regular length plus at most **one** extra semester; and
- no major disciplinary offense.

Because of the second condition, even one semester of probation removes Latin honors permanently, even if the student's cumulative GWA later rises above 3.80.

## 7.4 How the Honors Rules Connect to Scholarships

The Dean's List and the Bagwis Academic Excellence Scholarship (BAES) use the same GWA (3.50) and minimum grade (2.5), but they are not the same thing. The Dean's List needs only **15 units**, while the BAES needs at least **18 units**. A student with a GWA of 3.60 and 15 units is on the Dean's List but does **not** qualify for the BAES. See the Scholarships and Financial Aid Policy for the BAES rules.

# 8. Grade Appeals and Corrections

## 8.1 Appealing a Grade

A student who believes a final grade is wrong may appeal within **10 working days** after the grades are posted on BagwisNet. The steps are:

1. Talk to the instructor and ask for a review of the computation.
2. If not resolved, write to the department chair within the same 10 working days.
3. If still not resolved, write to the dean. The dean's decision is final.

## 8.2 Correction of Clerical Errors

If the Registrar finds a clerical error in a grade (for example, a typing mistake), the instructor submits a grade correction form signed by the dean. Corrections are reflected on BagwisNet within five working days. Academic standing and honors are recomputed after any correction.

# 9. Academic Integrity

## 9.1 Academic Dishonesty

Cheating, plagiarism, copying in examinations, submitting another person's work, and using unauthorized materials or tools in an assessment are major offenses.

| Offense | Sanction |
|---|---|
| First offense | 0.0 on the exam or requirement |
| Second offense | 0.0 in the course and 5 school days of suspension |
| Third offense | Dismissal from the university |

Any academic dishonesty sanction disqualifies the student from the Dean's List for that semester and from Latin honors. Due process follows the Student Handbook: written notice, **5 working days** to answer, and a hearing by the Student Discipline Board.

# 10. Graduation Requirements

To graduate, a student must:

- complete all courses in the curriculum with passing grades;
- have **no INC** on record by **March 20, 2027** (for the April 2027 graduation);
- file an application for graduation at the Registrar by **December 11, 2026**;
- pay the graduation fee of **₱2,500**;
- be cleared by all offices; and
- complete the exit interview at the Guidance and Counseling Center.

Commencement Exercises are on **April 17, 2027** at the Bagwis Gymnasium.
