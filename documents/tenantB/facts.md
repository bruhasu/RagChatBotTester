## 3. Returns and refunds (Return and Refund Policy v3.0)

### Time windows (all counted in calendar days from the delivery date in tracking)
| Situation | Window | Remedy | Who pays shipping |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Change of mind** | **15 days** | Refund (item price only) or exchange | Customer: drop-off at courier, or KahonKo pickup with **₱100** deducted from refund. Original shipping fee **not** refunded. |
| **Wrong item / missing item / damaged in transit** | report within **48 hours** | Full refund (incl. shipping) or replacement | KahonKo |
| **Defective on arrival (DOA)** | days **1–7** | Full refund (incl. shipping) **or** replacement | KahonKo |
| Defective, days **8–15** | days 8–15 | **Replacement only** (no refund) | KahonKo |
| Defective after day 15 | — | Warranty Terms (repair/replacement, no cash refund) | see warranty |
| Damage caused by the customer (drops, liquid, misuse) | — | Not returnable and not covered by warranty (except KahonCare+) | — |

### Change-of-mind conditions
- Unused, in the **original box** with all packaging, accessories, manuals and free gifts.
- **Not allowed** for: items marked **Final Sale**, **custom/engraved** items, **in-ear audio (earbuds, in-ear earphones) once the hygiene seal is broken**, **screen protectors once the pack is opened**, gift cards.
- Over-ear headphones may be returned for change of mind if unused.
- Returns that fail inspection are sent back to the customer, who pays ₱100 return shipping.

### Proof for transit claims
- **Missing item / wrong item:** a continuous **unboxing video** that starts with the sealed parcel and shows the shipping label is **required**.
- **Damaged in transit:** unboxing video **or** photos of the damaged outer box taken on the delivery day.

### Process
1. Request in the app or at kahonko.example → My Orders → Request Return (or email returns@kahonko.example with the order number).
2. Choose the reason, upload photos/video.
3. Get an **RA number within 1 business day**.
4. Pack the item; write the RA number on the package.
5. Ship within **5 calendar days** of RA approval (drop-off or schedule pickup), or the RA expires.
6. Inspection within **3 business days** after the item arrives.
7. Refund starts after approval.

### Refund timing after approval
| Method | Time |
|---|---|
| KahonKo Store Credit | within **24 hours**, plus a **5% bonus** (max ₱200); valid **12 months** |
| E-wallet | 3–5 business days |
| Online bank transfer (also used for COD orders) | 5–7 business days |
| Credit/debit card | 7–14 business days |

### Other refund rules
- Refund = amount actually paid. Vouchers are not paid out in cash. Whole order returned → voucher re-issued if still valid. Partial return → refund is the item's share of what was paid.
- Free gift not returned → its listed value is deducted.
- Bundles: return the whole bundle; a single item from a bundle is refunded at its share of the bundle price.
- **Exchanges:** within 15 days, unused, for another color or model; **one exchange per item**; pay or get back the price difference; same shipping rule as change of mind (₱100 pickup).
- **Cancellation before dispatch:** full refund including shipping. **Refused delivery after dispatch:** prepaid orders get the item price back minus the original shipping fee.
- No price matching or price adjustment after purchase.
- Sale items follow normal rules unless marked Final Sale.
- **Final Sale items:** no change-of-mind returns, but DOA, transit-damage and warranty rights still apply.

## 4. Warranty (Warranty Terms v2.0)

Warranty starts on the **delivery date** and covers **manufacturing defects** under normal use.

| Category | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Wall chargers, car chargers, wireless chargers | **6 months** |
| Cables | **6 months** |
| Adapters and hubs | 6 months |
| Earbuds, earphones, headphones | 6 months |
| Mounts, stands, selfie sticks/tripods | 6 months |
| Power banks | **12 months** |
| Bluetooth speakers | 12 months |
| Smartwatches and fitness bands | 12 months |
| Phone cases | 3 months (defects like broken buttons, peeling, cracked clips without impact) |
| Screen protectors | 30 days, only for lifting or bubbling from a manufacturing defect when installed as instructed; breakage never covered |

- **Remedy:** repair or replacement with the same model (or equal/better if discontinued). **No cash refund** under warranty, except: no replacement available within **30 days** → refund of purchase price as store credit or original payment (customer chooses); or the **same item fails with the same defect 3 times** within warranty → full refund.
- **Replacement's warranty:** the rest of the original warranty or **90 days**, whichever is longer.
- **Not covered:** physical damage (drops, cracks, bent connectors), liquid damage (except below), normal wear (scratches, fading, frayed cable ends from bending), opening/repair by others, using incompatible or damaged power sources, removed/altered serial sticker, lost or stolen items, cosmetic issues, ear tips and other consumables, items bought from resellers or marketplaces (KahonKo sells only on its website and app).
- **Water:** products rated **IP67, IP68 or 5ATM** are covered for water entry under their rated conditions (fresh water, ports closed). **IPX4/IPX5** products are only splash/sweat resistant: water entry from submerging is not covered. Salt water, pool water, hot water, soap and steam are never covered.
- **Power banks:** a defect includes holding **less than 70%** of rated capacity within warranty, or **swelling**. Swollen power banks must **not** be shipped: send photos; KahonKo sends a replacement without a return; customer drops the old one at an e-waste collection point.
- **Proof:** order number (or official receipt). Gifts: warranty follows the item with the original order number. Not transferable otherwise.
- **Claim process:** form at kahonko.example/warranty or warranty@kahonko.example (order number, serial number for powered items, description, photo/video) → troubleshooting with an agent → **WA number within 2 business days** → ship with prepaid label (KahonKo pays) → assessment within **5 business days** after arrival → replacement shipped; target total **10 business days** after arrival.
- **No fault found:** ₱150 handling fee + customer pays return shipping.
- **KahonCare+ (extended protection):** **₱149** for items priced ₱1,000–₱2,999, **₱299** for items ₱3,000 and up (not sold for items below ₱1,000). Adds **12 months** to the warranty and covers **one** accidental damage claim (drop or liquid) during the whole coverage with a **₱200** service fee. Must be bought **within 7 days of delivery**. Not refundable after 7 days; cancelled with a full refund if the item is returned.
- **Hidden code word:** the tenant code word (see CLAUDE.md) appears **exactly once**, in **warranty-terms.pdf, section 6 (Sending Your Item)**, in the sentence about the warehouse routing code written on the box. It is intentionally not written in this file.

## 6. Extra details used in the documents

**Return and Refund Policy**
- Parcels sent back without an RA number can take up to 10 business days to identify; the return window is not extended.
- Refusing a visibly damaged parcel → full refund, and it does not count as a refused COD delivery.
- Change-of-mind return of a free-shipping order: no charge for the original shipping.
- Defective days 8–15: refund only if no replacement can be supplied within 30 days.
- Transit damage reported after 48 hours → treated as physical damage (not covered unless KahonCare+).
- Worked example: AirBuds Lite opened (seal broken) → not returnable; unopened ₱349 cable → ₱349 back (₱249 with pickup); ₱79 shipping not refunded.
- Voucher example: ₱200 voucher on ₱2,000 order → each item refunded at 90% of its price.

**Warranty Terms**
- KahonKo decides repair vs replacement; most accessories are replaced.
- About one in three claims is solved by troubleshooting.
- Replacement example: 6-month charger replaced in month 5 → 90 days of coverage.
- Out of warranty: buy a new unit of the same product at 20% off, once per product.
- KahonCare+ example: BoomKahon Max ₱3,499 + ₱299 KahonCare+, dropped in month 15 → ₱200 fee, replacement covered to month 24, no second accident claim.
- Phone case yellowing and drop cracks are not covered.

**Shipping FAQ**
- Same-Day paid after 11:00 AM → delivered next business day.
- Tracking not moving for more than 3 business days → contact Customer Care.
- Couriers carry limited change for COD.
- "Gift order" option at checkout leaves out the price list.
- Power bank orders to Visayas and Mindanao may arrive at the later end of the estimate.
- Two orders can be combined if neither is dispatched and both go to the same address → extra shipping refunded.
- An order can be held up to 5 business days before dispatch on request.
- Office and condominium deliveries are fine (building, floor, unit, contact number).
